Fever Log privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Fever Log does not collect, receive or share any personal data. Everything you log stays on your phone.

What the app stores

The names of the people you add, temperature readings, medicine names, amounts, times and notes, and your settings, such as °C or °F. They are kept in a database inside the app, on your phone.

What we receive

Nothing. Fever Log has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends nothing over the internet.

Phone backups

Your phone's own backup, such as iCloud Backup or Google backup, may include the app's data. Those backups are stored under the terms of your Apple or Google account, and we have no access to them.

Export and import

Export creates a file of your entries. It goes only where you choose to send it from your phone's share sheet. Import reads only a file or text that you choose.

Links to other websites

The app links to NHS guidance on fever in children. That page opens in your browser, and the NHS website's own privacy policy applies there.

Deleting your data

Delete single entries, or a person with all their entries, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes its data from the phone. Copies in phone backups follow your backup settings.

Children

Fever Log is meant for parents and carers. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
