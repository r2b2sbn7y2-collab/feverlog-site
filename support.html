Fever Log support

Questions, problems or ideas: r2b2sbn7y2@privaterelay.appleid.com

Where is my data?

On your phone only. Fever Log has no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

How do I switch between °C and °F?

Tap the settings button at the top right, then choose the temperature unit. Readings you've already logged are converted for display.

How do I move entries from the Fever Log web page?

On the web page, tap Copy CSV. In the app, open Settings, tap Paste under Import, check the summary, then tap Import.

How do I back up or move my log to a new phone?

Your phone's own backup includes the app. You can also export a CSV from Settings and import it on the new phone.

How do I delete everything?

Delete each person under People, or delete the app.

Fever Log is a log, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before making medical decisions.
